Bella Speranza

The Dance of Hearts

A digital universe filled with inspiration for those who wish to create joy and presence through movement and music.

Here you will find free access to the book The Dance of Hearts, beautiful music pieces and inspiring videos based on the experiences from Bella Speranza.

Bella Speranza was an NGO project that ran from 2012 to 2020, created by volunteers at the Royal Danish Theatre to provide chronically ill children with a break from their illness.

Through dance, movement and music, children could express themselves freely and find strength in their bodies, regardless of physical limitations.

The Dance of Hearts was written to inspire others to bring people together through dance and music – in hospitals, nursing homes, schools, rehabilitation centres or anywhere there is a need for movement, community and joy of life.
